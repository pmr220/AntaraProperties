Materials & construction specifications
Structure
RCC framed structure with 6-inch external walls and 4-inch internal walls in red brickwork.
Doors
Decorative main door laminated on both sides. Granite frames with pinewood flush doors for toilets; plywood/granite frames and laminated pinewood flush doors elsewhere.
Kitchen
Granite platform, stainless-steel sink, modular kitchen trolley and chimney. Wall tiles up to lintel level.
Paint
Internal wall putty and plastic paint; premium acrylic paint externally.
Flooring
Premium vitrified tiles in rooms and passages, with optional wooden flooring. Toilet wall tiles up to lintel level; vitrified/ceramic common-passage tiles and natural stone/vitrified staircase steps.
Windows
UPVC sliding windows with mosquito nets and granite surrounds on all four sides.
Electrical
Concealed wiring and premium modular switches. AC points in bedrooms and hall; inverter points in each room. Solar electricity for common areas and balcony, as described in the brochure.
Plumbing
Concealed plumbing with hot/cold mixer or diverter and shower in each toilet; premium sanitary fittings.
Water storage
Common overhead and underground tanks with separate domestic and potable-water partitions and an electric motor.
Parking surface
Paving blocks, parking tiles or premix.
